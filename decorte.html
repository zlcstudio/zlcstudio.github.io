About

DECORTÉ is a Japanese luxury beauty house where skincare science meets refined, sensory elegance. For this photographic series, we interpreted the brand through a precise, dark visual language. Flowers become sculptural forms, moving between botanical still life and portraiture, while deep shadows and considered compositions bring a bold, atmospheric edge to its world of beauty.
