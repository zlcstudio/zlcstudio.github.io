About

Olya Rooftop is a restaurant in Algiers where Mediterranean cuisine, rooftop elevation and Algerian roots meet. The name bridges two worlds — ʿulyā (عليا) in Arabic meaning height and elevation, and oliva in Latin, evoking the olive and its naturalness.

We developed the full brand identity with XBM Studio: custom lettering, bilingual logo (Latin and Arabic), colour palette, iconography, packaging, signage and social media direction — building a visual language that is organic, warm and immediately recognisable.
