About

SHAPED BY PLACES.
DRIVEN BY CURIOSITY.

I’m Amina Zelaci, an art director and image-maker working under the name ZLC STUDIO.

Born in Algeria, I’ve lived in different places throughout my life. Moving between cultures, alongside travel, encounters and everything I discover along the way, continues to shape my perspective and creative approach.

I move between visual identity, art direction, photography and film, bringing different disciplines together to create worlds that feel both intentional and alive.

Now based in Paris, I’m available worldwide for brand partnerships and creative direction.
