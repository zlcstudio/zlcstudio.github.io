Services
Artistic photographic project
About

We presented this project at the end of the 2022 Tilawin mentorship. It began with a departure from Algeria to France and a mechanical shift in the 35mm film that brought three moments — Algiers, the flight and Paris — into one connected panoramic image. We turned the accident into a way to trace a presence across both countries, then continued the experiment through photographs shaped by the film's unpredictability.
