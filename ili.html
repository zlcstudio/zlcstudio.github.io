About

We created this magazine in collaboration with artist NOUN and XBM Studio for ILI, a 2024 exhibition by NOUN. Bringing together a broad selection of works shown in the exhibition, the publication extends the show into print and becomes a work in its own right — an exploration of existence and presence.
